# Participant information sheet and consent (draft, English)

> Shown on the first page of the survey. Translations into Chinese, German, Spanish and French will be prepared and
> checked by native speakers before data collection. Bracketed items are for the investigator.

## About this study

You are invited to take part in a study about how people think about minds: their own, and those of AI chatbots
such as ChatGPT. The study is run by Zijian Zeng at UCSI University, Malaysia, and has been approved by the UCSI
University Research Ethics Committee [reference number].

## What you will do

The survey takes about 10 minutes. You will answer short yes/no questions about yourself (for example, "Do you feel
hungry?"), read two short answers written by an AI chatbot, and then answer the same questions about AI chatbots.
There are no right or wrong answers.

## Possible risks and benefits

There are no known risks beyond those of everyday life. Some questions ask whether you feel things such as pain or
fear; you only answer yes or no. You will be paid [amount] through [Prolific / Credamo]. There is no other direct
benefit to you.

## Your data

We do not ask for your name or any information that identifies you. Your platform ID is used only to pay you and is
deleted before the data are analysed. The anonymous answers will be published openly so that other researchers can
check and reuse them.

## Taking part is voluntary

You may stop at any time by closing the survey, without giving a reason; incomplete responses are not used. Because
the answers are anonymous, we cannot remove your answers after you have submitted them.
