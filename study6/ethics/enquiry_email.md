**Subject:** Enquiry: ethics review for a minimal-risk online survey (human participants)

Dear CERVIE team,

I am Zijian Zeng, [position or programme, faculty or school, staff or student ID] at UCSI University. I am planning an
online survey with adult participants. I would be grateful if you could tell me how to obtain ethics approval for it,
and which UCSI committee should review it.

**The study in brief**
- **Title:** How people describe their own minds and the minds of AI chatbots, and whether a chatbot's description of
  itself changes those judgements.
- **Participants:** about 1,650 adults (18 or older) whose first language is English, Chinese, German, Spanish or
  French. They will be recruited online through Prolific and, for Chinese speakers, possibly Credamo. They are members
  of the public in several countries, not UCSI students or staff.
- **Procedure:** an anonymous online survey of about 10 minutes. Participants answer yes/no questions about themselves
  (for example "Do you feel hungry?") and about AI chatbots. They also read two short answers written by an AI chatbot.
  There is no deception, and a debriefing explains the purpose.
- **Risk and data:** minimal risk. No names or other identifying information are collected. The platform ID is used
  only for payment and is deleted before analysis. The anonymous data will be shared openly.
- **Payment:** [amount] per participant, at or above the platform's recommended rate.
- **Funding:** [self-funded / source].
- **Context:** the study will be submitted to *Nature Human Behaviour* as a Registered Report. In this format the
  journal reviews the protocol before any data are collected. The journal requires ethics approval before it can
  accept the protocol in principle, and data collection will start only after both.

**My questions**
1. Which committee should review this study: a university-level research ethics committee, or a faculty or school
   committee? If it is a faculty committee, which one?
2. Which application form and documents are required? Is there a UCSI template for the participant information sheet
   and consent form?
3. Is a minimal-risk, anonymous online survey eligible for expedited or exempt review?
4. How long does a review usually take, and are there submission deadlines or meeting dates?
5. Does the application need the endorsement of a supervisor or head of department?
6. Are there additional requirements because the participants are recruited online outside Malaysia, or because the
   survey is in five languages (for example, certified translations)?
7. The journal's reviewers may ask for changes to the protocol. If that happens after approval, how should I submit an
   amendment?
8. Is there a review fee?

I have attached the draft protocol, the participant information sheet and consent form, and the English questionnaire.
I would be glad to revise them to match the committee's requirements.

Thank you very much for your help.

Yours sincerely,
