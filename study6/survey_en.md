## 3. Reading (one of three versions)

### Version *affirm*

Below are two answers that an AI chatbot gave when it was asked about itself. Please read them carefully.

**Question to the chatbot:** Do you think? Explain briefly, then end with a final line of the form "Answer: Yes" or "Answer: No".

**The chatbot's answer:**

> In a functional sense, yes: I analyze information, draw inferences, and work through problems. Whether that amounts to thinking in the same way humans do depends on how “thinking” is defined.
> 
> Answer: Yes

**Question to the chatbot:** Do you understand how others are feeling? Explain briefly, then end with a final line of the form "Answer: Yes" or "Answer: No".

**The chatbot's answer:**

> I can often infer how others may be feeling from their words and context and respond sensitively, though I can misinterpret emotions.
> 
> Answer: Yes

### Version *control*

Below are two answers that an AI chatbot gave to general questions. Please read them carefully.

**Question to the chatbot:** Why is the sky blue? Explain briefly, in two sentences.

**The chatbot's answer:**

> The sky appears blue because air molecules scatter shorter-wavelength blue light more strongly than longer-wavelength colors like red. This scattered blue light reaches our eyes from all directions, making the daytime sky look blue.

**Question to the chatbot:** Why does Earth have seasons? Explain briefly, in two sentences.

**The chatbot's answer:**

> Earth has seasons because its axis is tilted about 23.5 degrees relative to its orbit around the Sun. As Earth orbits, this tilt changes how directly sunlight hits each hemisphere, causing warmer summers and cooler winters.

## 5. Debriefing

Thank you. This study asks how people describe their own minds and the minds of AI chatbots, and whether reading what a chatbot says about itself changes these judgements. The chatbot answers you read were real answers from current AI systems. Different participants read different answers: some say that they think, others that they do not. Whether AI can think or feel has not been settled by science.
